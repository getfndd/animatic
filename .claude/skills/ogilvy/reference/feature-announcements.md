# Feature Announcement Framework

Structure for launch and release copy — what to lead with, what to cut. Load for `@ogilvy announce`.

---

When a feature ships, announce it with substance, not hype.

### Announcement Structure

1. **Headline**: What changed + why it matters (not "Introducing..." or "We're excited to announce...")
2. **The problem**: What was painful before. Be specific.
3. **The solution**: What the feature does. Mechanism, not marketing.
4. **The proof**: Screenshot, GIF, or specific outcome.
5. **Who benefits**: Name the persona and use case.
6. **What to do**: Specific CTA. Not "check it out" — "Open your data room and click Organize."

### Announcement Anti-Patterns

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| "We're excited to announce..." | "Your data room now organizes itself." |
| "Introducing our new AI feature" | "Upload a term sheet. Get a plain-English summary in 30 seconds." |
| "We've been working hard on..." | [Skip this. Nobody cares about your process.] |
| "This is just the beginning" | [Ship what's done. Promise nothing.] |
| Feature laundry list | One headline feature + supporting details |
