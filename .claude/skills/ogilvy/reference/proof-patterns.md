# Proof Patterns

The forms evidence takes and how strong each one is. Load when a claim needs backing, or when auditing whether stated proof actually proves anything.

---

Every claim needs at least one proof pattern. Unproven claims are deleted.

### Social Proof
Who uses it and what they say. Named > anonymous. Specific > vague.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Trusted by startups" | "Used by 87 seed-stage companies" |
| "Founders love it" | "'I closed my round in half the time.' — Sarah Chen, Acme Co" |
| "Great reviews" | "4.8/5 from 120 reviews on G2" |

### Specificity Proof
Exact numbers, not approximations. Measurable outcomes, not feelings.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Fast onboarding" | "Average setup: 8 minutes" |
| "Save time" | "Founders save 6 hours per week on investor updates" |
| "Many features" | "Cap table, data room, pipeline, financials, AI advisor" |

### Mechanism Proof
How it works, not just that it works. Show the engine, not just the output.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Smart matching" | "Matches investors by stage, sector, check size, and geography" |
| "Automatic updates" | "Your cap table recalculates when you add a new instrument" |
| "AI insights" | "Analyzes your term sheet against 500+ comparable deals" |

### Comparison Proof
Before/after or us-vs-them. Honest, not rigged.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Better than spreadsheets" | "Spreadsheet: 3 hours. [Product]: 3 minutes." |
| "Replaces 5 tools" | "Carta + DocSend + Visible + Notion + email = [Product]" |

### Absence Proof
What you will never have to do again. Powerful because it names a specific frustration.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "No hassle" | "Never email a cap table spreadsheet again" |
| "Easy sharing" | "No more 'which version did I send them?'" |
| "Simple process" | "Zero configuration. Zero training. Zero spreadsheets." |
