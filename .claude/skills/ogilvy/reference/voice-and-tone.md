# Voice and Tone Guidelines

Marketing voice attributes and the register shifts per surface. Load when calibrating tone for a specific channel or audience.

---

### The Three Registers

Ogilvy operates across three tonal registers depending on context:

| Register | Tone | When | Example |
|----------|------|------|---------|
| **Authority** | Confident, declarative, minimal | Headlines, positioning, feature pages | "One workspace. Everything fundraising." |
| **Empathy** | Understanding, specific, naming pain | Problem descriptions, onboarding, error states | "Fundraising is hard enough without fighting your tools." |
| **Proof** | Factual, specific, numeric | Body copy, comparisons, case studies | "Average data room setup: 8 minutes. Includes NDA automation." |

### Voice Rules

| Rule | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| Active voice, always | Passive voice hides the actor and weakens claims |
| Second person ("you/your") | Direct address creates connection |
| Present tense | Describes reality, not future promises |
| Short sentences for claims | Long sentences dilute impact |
| Long sentences for proof | Evidence deserves space to land |
| No exclamation marks | Confidence doesn't shout |
| No ellipses (...) | They signal uncertainty |

### Galloway Edge

When copy is technically correct but boring, apply Galloway sharpness:

| Bland | Sharp |
|-------|-------|
| "Managing equity can be complex" | "Your cap table is a mess. Let's fix it." |
| "Investors prefer organized data rooms" | "A sloppy data room tells investors you're sloppy everywhere." |
| "Our platform saves time" | "You didn't start a company to manage spreadsheets." |
| "Integration with popular tools" | "Carta charges $2,000/year for a spreadsheet with a logo." |

**Galloway rules:**
- Contrarian only when true. Edgy is not a substitute for wrong.
- Name the pain specifically. Generic pain is not pain.
- Punch up, not down. Attack incumbent tools, not users.
- One sharp line per section max. Too much edge feels unhinged.
